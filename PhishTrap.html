Manual Scam Identification Checklist

Use these verification rules when evaluating any job offer or rental agreement manually:

  • Check the Official Domain: Never trust an email ending in `@gmail.com` or an offer letter link on a strange domain. Real companies use exact corporate domains.
  • Verify via Careers Portal: Go directly to the official company website, navigate to their "Careers" page, and search for the job ID.
  • Beware Reverse Money Traps: If someone offers to send you money or asks for your bank details / ID to "give you joining bonus cash", it is a mule account trap.
